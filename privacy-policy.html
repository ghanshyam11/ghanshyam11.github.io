Swipe Quiz Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains how Swipe Quiz (Android package com.kg.swipequiz) handles information when you use the app.

Information handled by the app

Swipe Quiz is intended for users aged 13 and above and works without creating an account. The app does not ask you to enter your name, phone number, email address, or precise location. Quiz progress is stored locally rather than sent to a developer-operated server. Advertising services process additional information as explained below.

The app saves your selected language and quiz progress, including current levels, stars, and hints, in local storage on your device. This information is used to remember your preferences and let you continue learning.

Advertising and third-party data processing

Swipe Quiz uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display rewarded advertisements. You can choose to watch an available ad to earn an additional hint. The SDK initializes and requests an ad in advance, so advertising-related data processing can occur before you choose to watch an ad. Advertising requires an internet connection; quiz questions are bundled with the app.

Google's advertising SDK automatically collects and shares information for advertising, measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention. Depending on your device, permissions, and applicable settings, this can include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location.
  • Device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other identifiers where available.
  • Interactions with the app and ads, such as app launches, ad views, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash logs, performance information, and other technical data.

Ad requests transmit information to Google and may involve advertising partners used to deliver or measure ads. Ads may be personalized depending on applicable permissions, privacy choices, and advertising settings. We do not operate a separate quiz analytics service or sell your locally saved quiz progress.

For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your advertising and privacy choices

You can choose not to watch rewarded ads. This does not prevent the SDK from initializing or requesting an ad in advance. Your device may provide controls to reset or delete an advertising identifier or limit advertising personalization; availability and menu names depend on your operating system.

On iOS, the app requests tracking authorization through Apple's system permission prompt. You can review or change this permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Declining tracking permission does not stop all advertising-related data processing.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Saved progress and preferences remain in the app's local storage until they are reset or the app's data is cleared. You can reset quiz progress using the app's reset option. To remove all locally stored app data on Android, open Settings, select Apps, choose Swipe Quiz, open Storage (or Storage & cache), and select Clear storage (or Clear data). Device menu names may vary.

We do not maintain a server-side copy of your quiz progress. Any device backup or restore behavior depends on your operating system and backup settings.

Clearing the app's local data does not delete information already processed by Google or its advertising partners. Their retention practices and available privacy controls are described in their respective policies. For requests concerning information you send us directly, contact the email address below.

Children's privacy

Swipe Quiz is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can investigate and address the request. Advertising-related processing is described above; this policy does not mean all users aged 13 and above are adults.
